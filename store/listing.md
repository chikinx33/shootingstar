## en-US

**App name (≤30):** Purrtectors: Cat Sky Squad

**Short description (≤80):** Guardian cats fly tiny fighters to secretly protect their humans' town!

**Full description:**

While their humans sleep, the guardian cats take to the sky.

Purrtectors is a cute vertical shooter where brave cats pilot round little fighters to defend their neighbourhood from a silly alien vacuum empire — without their humans ever noticing.

★ Fly, dodge, grow
Drag to fly and your cat fires automatically. Collect gems to level up mid-flight and pick illustrated skill cards: every weapon grows through 3 upgrades and 3 evolutions into a dazzling ultimate form.

★ Build your own fighter
Fit gear on the nose, armor, wings and engines, mix set bonuses, and arm the wing guns, wing missiles, tail mortar and a support core. Every part you equip appears on your craft.

★ Protect the whole neighbourhood
Guard house after house — the grandpa's deck, the corner store, the vet clinic — then face big bosses with readable bullet patterns.

★ Chick sidekicks
Rescue fire, nurse and pot-lid chicks that fight beside you.

★ Play your way
No account needed. Optional online account for cloud saves and guilds.

Made for short sessions on your phone — one sortie, one more house kept safe.
